# 🚜 KrishiMitra

### AI-Powered Farm Machinery Rental & Smart Matching Platform

> **Find the Right Farm Machinery, Right When You Need It.**

KrishiMitra is an AI-powered agricultural machinery rental platform that connects farmers with nearby machinery owners. It helps farmers discover, compare, AI-match, book, track, and rate farm machinery based on availability, price, distance, and reliability.

---

## 🌾 Problem Statement

Farmers often depend on phone calls, personal contacts, and local networks to find agricultural machinery such as tractors, JCBs, harvesters, rotavators, and seed drills.

This creates several challenges:

- Difficulty finding suitable machinery nearby
- Multiple phone calls to check availability
- Unclear or inconsistent rental prices
- No easy way to compare different machines
- Delays during critical farming periods
- Difficulty finding machinery during urgent situations
- Unnecessary travel and coordination effort
- Limited visibility into machinery owners and reliability

---

## 💡 Our Solution

KrishiMitra provides a single digital platform connecting **farmers and machinery owners**.

Farmers can enter their requirements such as:

- 📍 Location
- 🌱 Farm area
- 🔧 Type of agricultural work
- 🚜 Required machinery
- 📅 Date
- ⏱️ Duration
- 💰 Budget

The platform then discovers suitable nearby machinery and uses an **AI-powered smart matching system** to rank the best options.

---

## 🤖 AI Smart Matching

KrishiMitra ranks machinery according to multiple factors:

| Factor | Weight |
|--------|--------|
| Availability | 40% |
| Price | 25% |
| Distance | 20% |
| Reliability | 15% |

The system can also consider **work suitability and farm-size suitability** when generating recommendations.

Instead of simply showing a list of machines, KrishiMitra explains **why a particular machine is recommended**.

### Example

**94% AI Match**

- Availability: 100%
- Price: 90%
- Distance: 95%
- Reliability: 92%

> **Why this machine?**  
> Available on your required date, within your preferred budget, close to your farm, and highly rated by previous users.

## 🚨 Emergency Machinery Request

During situations such as:

- 🌧️ Sudden rainfall
- 🌾 Urgent harvesting
- 🚜 Machinery breakdown
- ⏰ Time-sensitive farming operations

farmers can send an **Emergency Machinery Request**.

The request can be broadcast to nearby available machinery owners to help find a suitable machine quickly.
